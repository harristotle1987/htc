Introduction

Aegis Vault ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by Aegis Vault.

Information We Collect

When you use Google Workspace integrations (such as Gmail), we ask for specific OAuth scopes to read and send email on your behalf. We only access the data absolutely necessary to provide the features of Aegis Vault. We do not store your emails on our servers permanently; they are processed directly to provide CRM context.

We use the information we collect to operate, maintain, and provide the features of our service. Specifically, Google user data accessed via OAuth is used exclusively to display correspondence history and enable email sending directly within your CRM pipeline.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share your Google User Data with third-party tools (including AI models) without your explicit consent. Aegis Vault's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
